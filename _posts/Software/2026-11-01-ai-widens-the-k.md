ai is going to widen the k shaped economy, so sell to the people at the top of the K, or sell the people at the bottom a way up

where does k shaped come from?
- after covid the recovery split in two like the letter K
	- people who own assets (stocks, houses) shot up
	- people living on wages fell behind
- its back now for consumer spending
	- the top 10% of earners now do ~half of all us consumer spending, up from ~35% in the early 90s
	- the bottom 80% fell to ~37%, from ~42% before covid
- asset prices went way up and inflation and high rates hit everyone else
	- so asset owners feel rich and keep spending, everyone else trades down
	- hermes and walmart growing, kohls and bath and body works hurting

how does ai change this game?
- ai benefits high capability individuals and mega corps with capital, so they pull away even faster
- we expect the top percentile to run massive agent fleets
- everything in the middle dies and the rest is left behind

what does this mean?
- sell to the asset owners
	- thats mostly older people and retirees, they own the houses and stocks
- sell to the people with ai tailwinds
	- the people running agents
	- and the agents themselves, as they're the ones doing the buying
- sell shovels to the people without these tailwinds trying to escape
	- e.g gambling and to dying businesses
- avoid markets where the demand side is collapsing
