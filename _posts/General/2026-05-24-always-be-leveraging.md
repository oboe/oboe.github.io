If you want to make a bigger impact on the face of the world having a more principled way of thinking about it will help.

At the end of the day theres two things which matter, how much **value** your action provides and how many times you can perform this action, the **leverage**.

My impression is that in many domains most people are positive value and will produce value, often in many domains just introducing change will result in a positive outcome half of the time.

And so the far less under-appreciated component is the leverage side.

Currently I think theres four main ways people gain leverage:
- Capital: you borrow money to increase the size of your bet
- Human: you use other people to increase the size of your bet
- Software: you use computers to run your value producing software multiple times
- Media: you duplicate your value producing media multiple times
